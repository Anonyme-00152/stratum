Why we built Stratum

Billions of dollars. Too few who can navigate them.

Environmental markets channel billions of dollars toward forest protection, clean energy and community development in the places that need it most. The problem is that navigating them requires deep expertise, proprietary data and relationships that take years to build.

We built Stratum so organisations don't have to navigate this alone. Our platform, our intelligence and our team sit at the centre of the market. We help buyers procure with confidence, investors find opportunities, and developers get their projects to the right buyers.
