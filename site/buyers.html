Why Stratum

Procurement trusted by market leaders

Transparent, competitive and conflict-free — a portfolio that balances cost, risk and impact.

01

Access to the whole market

We take your requirements to the entire market and reach projects you wouldn't find through standard channels.

02

A conflict-free, no-inventory model

No credits on our balance sheet and no financial position in any project or developer. Every recommendation is about your portfolio.

03

A standardised process, built for efficiency

Faster sourcing, easier comparison and a single contract covering all suppliers — tailored to you.

One partner. Better results.

Competition, not duplication, lowers your price

Approaching multiple brokers artificially inflates demand — and prices rise to match. We run one structured process: every relevant supplier bids for the same project, on the same terms, through the same platform.

Suppliers know the process is competitive and the buyer is serious, so they come in at their best price.
