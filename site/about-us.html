About us

We work on some of the most critical challenges on Earth

Climate, nature and people — and the markets that fund them. We built Stratum so no organisation has to navigate those markets alone.

Meet the team Talk to us
Our mission

To empower every organisation to build a thriving future for climate, nature, and people.

Conflict-free by design

No inventory, no developer fees. We hold ourselves to the standards we set for the market.

Backed by climate investors

Funded by investors who share our long-term view of environmental markets.

Science-led

Every recommendation is grounded in peer-reviewed methods and real transaction data.

Why we built Stratum

Billions of dollars. Too few who can navigate them.

Environmental markets channel billions of dollars toward forest protection, clean energy and community development in the places that need it most. The problem is that navigating them requires deep expertise, proprietary data and relationships that take years to build.

We built Stratum so organisations don't have to navigate this alone. Our platform, our intelligence and our team sit at the centre of the market. We help buyers procure with confidence, investors find opportunities, and developers get their projects to the right buyers.
