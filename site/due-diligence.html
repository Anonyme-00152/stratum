FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Ask us something else
Can you assess a project that hasn't been rated by a third party?

Yes. Many high-value projects carry no public rating, which can rule them out by default. Our framework applies to any project where you know the country, developer and methodology — opening up options others overlook.

How do I know a project on the ground matches its documents?

Our Geo screening tool uses satellite data to check land eligibility, deforestation history, hazard exposure and baseline validity, so you can validate what's on paper against what's on the ground.

What are the five risk pillars?

Project quality, methodology, policy environment, developer track record and market conditions. Each is scored with clear evidence so decisions are auditable.

Can due diligence be bought on its own?

Yes. Any level can be commissioned independently of a procurement — useful for existing portfolios, investment theses or supplier-led offers.
