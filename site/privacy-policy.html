Privacy Policy

Stratum is a fictional company created as a portfolio concept.

Stratum is a fictional company created as a portfolio concept. This page shows how a privacy policy would be presented; no personal data is collected or stored by this demo site.

1. Who we are

Stratum Markets Ltd ("Stratum", "we") would be the controller of personal data collected through this website.

2. Data we would collect

  • Contact details you submit through forms (name, work email, company, message).
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited, only with your consent.

3. How we would use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or send a report you requested.
  • To send our newsletter, if you opted in.
  • To improve the website, using aggregated analytics.

4. Legal bases

Consent (newsletter, analytics), legitimate interests (responding to business enquiries) and contract (providing services to clients).

5. Retention

We would keep enquiry data for up to 24 months unless a business relationship follows, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

7. Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics cookies would only be set if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner. In this demo, your choice is stored only in your browser.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
