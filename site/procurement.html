The process

How we execute market-leading RFPs with you

Step 1 · Build your requirements

We define what you need before a single supplier is contacted

Good procurement starts with a clear brief. Our team translates your climate commitments, risk tolerance and budget into procurement criteria the market can respond to. We hold no credits and have no inventory to push, so every decision is shaped by your requirements, not ours.

You get access to
  • Best-in-class industry benchmarks
  • Bespoke procurement guidance
  • Internal education and stakeholder engagement
Step 2 · Access the largest VCM database

Reach the entire market with a single invitation

Our network includes 1,300+ vetted project developers across every major registry and project type. We broadcast your RFP to eligible suppliers only and handle onboarding and submission support. Projects that miss your minimum criteria are filtered out before they reach your desk.

You get access to
  • 18,000+ projects in our database
  • Outreach to 1,300+ active suppliers
  • Developer onboarding and technical support
Step 3 · Run a structured RFP

One standardised process, customised to your needs

Collect comparable proposals at scale. You define the questions that matter; we provide a validated, standardised data framework that keeps submissions consistent and easy to benchmark — reviewed in a dashboard you can filter, sort and export.

You get access to
  • Customisable RFP questionnaires
  • Dynamic proposal dashboards
  • Multi-stage support
Step 4 · Evaluate with confidence

Embedded risk screening at multiple levels

Automated screening runs a rapid first pass on reputation, ratings, methodology risk and country exposure. Analyst-led due diligence then goes deeper across our five risk pillars for the projects that matter most — with an auditable record for every decision.

You get access to
  • Level 1 — Automated risk screening
  • Level 2 — Analyst-led due diligence
  • Level 3 — Advanced due diligence
  • Across all — Geo screening
Step 5 · Build your portfolio

Compare and optimise before you commit

Our portfolio constructor lets you stress-test combinations across price, vintage, geography, project type and risk. We help you land a recommended portfolio that is straightforward to defend to sustainability leads, procurement teams and CFOs.

You get access to
  • Portfolio constructor
  • Scenario modelling
  • Presentation-ready outputs
Step 6 · Purchase through one contract

Centralise your spend at competitive prices

We act as your contracting counterparty: you sign one contract with Stratum instead of managing separate agreements with multiple developers. We handle negotiation, commercial terms and execution — from spot to long-term forward offtakes.

You get access to
  • Supplier negotiation
  • Single consolidated contracts
  • Centralised documentation

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Ask us something else
How long does a typical RFP take?

Because sourcing, evaluation and contracting live in one workflow, competitive procurements run in weeks rather than months. Timelines depend on volume, criteria and the number of stages. Contracting follows once you have approved a recommended portfolio.

Do we have to buy through Stratum?

No. You can run your programme independently on the platform, or let our team manage it end to end. When we act as contracting counterparty, you sign one contract instead of many.

How do you get paid if you don't take a margin on credits?

Our compensation is separated from the price you pay suppliers, and we never take fees from developers. That keeps our incentives aligned with yours: the best portfolio at the best price.

Can we include removals, avoidance and EACs in the same RFP?

Yes. Multi-lot RFPs are common, and our framework normalises submissions so you can compare across project types, vintages and geographies.

What contract structures do you support?

Spot purchases, forward purchases and long-term offtakes, including delivery guarantees and replacement mechanisms defined up front.
