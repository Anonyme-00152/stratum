Market challenges

Built for the VCM's biggest challenges. Trusted by companies that move markets.

The challenge

Most buyers only see projects brought by a handful of brokers, which narrows options and skews pricing.

How we solve it

We run a structured, transparent process across our developer network, so you see real competing offers and can benchmark live prices.

The challenge

Project quality varies and standards keep evolving, making it hard to know what holds up.

How we solve it

Our experts run fully independent, tiered due diligence on the projects that matter to you, from screening to advanced assessment.

The challenge

Much of the advice on offer comes from intermediaries earning commission on what they recommend.

How we solve it

We don't take positions in projects or earn developer commissions. We're only accountable to you.

The challenge

Procurement teams stay cautious because a bad project can land them in the headlines.

How we solve it

Every project is screened across our five-pillar framework, flagging country, methodology, counterparty, project and additionality risks — and monitored over time.

The challenge

Manual procurement means months of spreadsheets, email threads and bilateral negotiations.

How we solve it

Our RFP platform pulls sourcing, evaluation and contracting into one workflow, so procurements run in weeks rather than months.

The challenge

CFOs and boards want hard evidence on price, quality and impact for every credit.

How we solve it

We give you the evidence pack: pricing benchmarks, due diligence reports and impact reporting that holds up to board-level scrutiny.

The challenge

Long-term supply is exposed to prices and rules that can move within a single budget cycle.

How we solve it

We structure multi-year offtakes with transparent pricing and rebalance them as the market evolves.
