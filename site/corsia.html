Two-level eligibility

A credit only counts if both levels pass. We assess both.

Our funnel approach screens each eligibility criterion separately, so you can prioritise the projects most likely to qualify.

01

Programme & vintage

Is the project issued under an ICAO-approved programme, with an eligible vintage and methodology?

02

Host-country authorisation

Has the host country issued a Letter of Authorisation and committed to a corresponding adjustment?

03

Guarantee & insurance

Is the unit protected against double-counting and authorisation revocation?

04

Delivery risk

Can the developer deliver the volume in time for your compliance deadline?
